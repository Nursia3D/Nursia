## Overview
This page describes Nursia samples.

## Samples
Name|Description
----|-----------
[Tutorial](https://github.com/Nursia3D/Nursia/tree/main/Samples/Nursia.Samples.Tutorial)|Sample made in [Quick Start Tutorial](../index.md)
[FuelCell](https://github.com/Nursia3D/Nursia/tree/main/Samples/Nursia.Samples.FuelCell)|Modified MonoGame sample that uses Nursia for rendering. Original sample could be found here: https://github.com/MonoGame/MonoGame.Samples
[Water](https://github.com/Nursia3D/Nursia/tree/main/Samples/Nursia.Samples.Water)|Sample demonstrating usage of Terrain and Water
[ThirdPerson](https://github.com/Nursia3D/Nursia/tree/main/Samples/Nursia.Samples.ThirdPerson)|Sample demonstrating simple third person movement through the skeletal animation. Also it demonstrates creation of custom SceneNode(ReflectionPlane) and custom material(PlaneReflectMaterial)
[RacingDemo](https://github.com/Nursia3D/Nursia.RacingDemo)|Modified XNA sample that was adopted for Nursia. It's complicated sample demonstrating whole variety of features: subscenes, custom material(SimpleGlassMaterial), etc
